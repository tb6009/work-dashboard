아래는 본 연구의 연구질문을 번역한 것입니다. 선행논문의 직접 인용이나 응답자가 답할 추가 설문문항이 아닙니다.

RQ0. How are perceived AI productivity contributions, job expansion, and work intensification distributed and experienced jointly among designers with different lengths of professional experience?

RQ1-1. How is generative AI use related to task crafting, and does this relationship vary with generative AI literacy?

RQ1-2. How is task crafting related to perceived AI productivity contributions, job expansion, and work intensification, and does each relationship vary with occupational expertise?

RQ1-3. What direct and conditional indirect associations link generative AI use to each outcome through task crafting?

RQ2-1. How is perceived AI replacement threat related to generative AI use and skill crafting, and how is generative AI use related to skill crafting?

RQ2-2. Does the relationship between perceived AI replacement threat and generative AI use vary with generative AI literacy?

RQ2-3. How is skill crafting related to perceived AI productivity contributions, job expansion, and work intensification, and does each relationship vary with occupational expertise?

RQ2-4. What direct and conditional indirect associations link perceived AI replacement threat, generative AI use, skill crafting, and the three outcomes?

RQ-I. When task crafting and skill crafting are considered jointly for the same respondents, how is each uniquely associated with the three outcomes, and how do their coefficients differ?

RQ-E. What patterns emerge in the joint distribution of work outcomes, and how do these patterns relate to voluntary and imposed expansion experiences and tool-use and work contexts?
