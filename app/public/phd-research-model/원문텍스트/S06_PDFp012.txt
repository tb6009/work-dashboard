                                This study conducted exploratory factor analysis (EFA) on the 311 data points using
                         SPSS 27.0. The results indicated a Kaiser–Meyer–Olkin (KMO) value of 0.915, which exceeds
                            the recommended threshold of 0.7. Bartlett’s Test of Sphericity yielded a chi-square value
                              of 5244.240 (df = 136), which was significant at the 0.1% level, indicating that the data are
                               suitable for factor analysis. Principal component analysis was used to extract factors, with
                           oblique rotation, and factors with eigenvalues greater than 1 were retained. The results
                             of the EFA showed that the five extracted factors accounted for a cumulative variance of
                           89.746%, surpassing the 60% threshold. These five dimensions were: basic operational
                                     skills, prompt engineering ability, innovative application ability, quality evaluation ability,
                       and compliance and ethical awareness. As shown in Table 2, the standardized factor
                            loadings for all measurement items ranged from 0.82 to 0.92, all exceeding the 0.5 threshold,
                             indicating good construct validity for the items.
