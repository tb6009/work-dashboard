Designers’ Generative AI Use and Work Experience — English Questionnaire v0.8

This study examines designers’ use of generative AI, work behaviors, and occupational experiences. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time. There are no right or wrong answers. Please report experiences of no change or negative change as they occurred. Do not provide identifying information, such as an organization name, client name, or contact details.

Generative AI refers to functions that generate or revise text, images, video, audio, or code in response to instructions. Manual editing in conventional software alone does not count.
