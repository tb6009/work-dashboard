Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: A5 landscape infographic background — 1748 x 1240 px at 150 dpi

Format:
Wide horizontal composition for an A5 landscape editorial infographic. Image is a background layer beneath precise HTML/SVG typography.

Subject:
A refined abstract physical content-production system: four distinct modular stations connected by one precise electric-blue rail. Blank white editorial cards move through the stations and emerge as one neatly aligned finished card. The visual metaphor is strategy, generation, evidence-based filtering, and accumulated reusable assets. No people.

Composition:
Keep the entire left 42 percent visually quiet, nearly white and uncluttered, reserved for title and lead typography. Place the four modular stations in a controlled horizontal sequence across the middle-right and lower-right. Use a few blank paper cards and machined matte geometric components. Clear separation between four stages. Electric blue appears only as thin connectors, tiny edge accents, and one small structural marker. No large blue areas. Strict orthogonal alignment and generous negative space.

Brand style:
Swedish industrial engineering brand. Precise, restrained, technical. Clean white or very light neutral ground with generous negative space. A single saturated electric blue (#0000FE) used sparingly as a structural accent — thin rules, edges, and key marks only, never as a large flooded field. Everything else in neutral greys and near-black. Geometric, orthogonal, machined feeling. Sharp edges, even line weights, no ornament.

Lighting and material:
Even, diffuse, neutral daylight. Matte surfaces. Real machined metal where metal appears. No dramatic rim light, no lens flare, no glow.

Reference direction:
technical documentation photography, editorial process visualization, modular design system, matter-of-fact, high fidelity physical materials, low information density

Avoid:
any Korean text, any Hangul characters, any rendered lettering or labels, any numbers or measurements or part designations, any logo or wordmark or brandmark, any SKF logo, gradients, glossy or plastic surfaces, 3D render look, glowing edges, neon, lens flare, bokeh, drop shadows, stock-photo businesspeople, handshake imagery, generic AI-tech visuals such as glowing brains or circuit boards or floating holograms, busy backgrounds, watermark, signature, clutter.
한계와 외부 공개 전 확인동일 프롬프트를 다시 실행해도 장치 형태는 달라질 수 있습니다. SKF 블루 #0000FE 외 중성 팔레트·그리드·Pretendard는 provisional입니다. 스타일 라이브러리의 학생 작품·수업·시설 이미지는 워크플로 테스트용이며, 실제 홍보 게시 전 고해상도 원본과 작품 사용권·초상권·교과 최신성을 담당 부서가 재확인해야 합니다.
